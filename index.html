02 / A little about me

Good work starts with good questions.

I work at the intersection of product, brand, and the wonderfully messy human stuff in between. Over the last eight years, I’ve partnered with early-stage teams and established names to make digital products easier to understand and more rewarding to use.

My process is collaborative, hands-on, and grounded in listening. Away from the screen, I’m usually at a ceramics studio, looking for a very specific pastry, or walking the long way home.
